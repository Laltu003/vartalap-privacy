Privacy Policy for Vartalap

Last updated: November 2026

Vartalap is a messaging app made by Laltu Gorai in Jamshedpur, India. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, username, display name, a Vartalap ID (for example VT-XXXXXX), a profile photo and bio if you add them, and your password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Messages and media: text messages, photos, videos, files, voice notes, reactions, and the chats they belong to. Messages are stored so they can be delivered to the people you chat with.
  • Stories: photos and videos you post, the people allowed to see them (your chat partners at the time you post), and who has viewed them. Stories are hidden 24 hours after posting.
  • Calls: call records (who called whom, when, the type of call, whether it was answered, and its length). Call audio and video travel directly between devices, or through a relay server when a direct connection isn't possible. They are not recorded or stored by Vartalap.
  • Presence and settings: whether you are online, your last-seen time, read-receipt and privacy choices, theme and chat wallpaper preferences, and your blocked contacts.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send you message alerts. It is removed when you delete your account.
  • Reports: if you report a person or a story, the reason you chose and the person's account ID.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and record videos for chats and stories, and for voice and video calls. Used only when you choose these features.
  • Photos and videos: to show your recent gallery items when you add to your story, and to send the media you pick. Used only when you open those features.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages and calls.
  • Internet and vibration: to send and receive messages and to vibrate for alerts.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and to sign you in
  • To deliver messages, stories, calls and notifications
  • To let other people find you by username or Vartalap ID
  • To send a password-reset code by email when you ask for one
  • To handle reports and to remove content and accounts that break our rules

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, and Vartalap shows no ads.

4. Who we share information with

Vartalap uses these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, the database, and notifications)
  • Cloudinary (storage for photos and videos you send or post)
  • Brevo (sends password-reset emails)
  • Cloudflare (runs the server functions for notifications, password reset, sign-in lookups, and account deletion)
  • Open Relay Project (a relay server that carries call audio and video when a direct connection isn't possible)

Other users see your display name, username, Vartalap ID, profile photo, bio, online status and last-seen time, as your settings allow. They see your messages and stories only where they are part of the chat or the story's audience. Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Where and how long we keep information

  • Account, profile and messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Stories are hidden 24 hours after you post them. The story record and its media are removed when you delete your account.
  • Password-reset codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored only in hashed form.
  • Reports are kept so we can review them.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

By email: write to gorailaltu65@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for deletion. We will act within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, your private email record, your notification token, your stories, and the messages you sent. You are removed from your chats, and the chats stay for the other people in them. Your sign-in account is deleted. Photos and videos already uploaded to our media storage are deleted when the account is deleted, where the storage provider allows it. Backups held by our service providers are removed on their normal schedules.

7. Your choices

  • Turn read receipts, online status and last-seen time on or off in Settings
  • Block a contact, which stops them from messaging or calling you
  • Report a contact or a story
  • Choose which permissions to allow in your phone's settings

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access to other people's data is limited by security rules, and the app does not allow one user to read another user's private information. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

9. Children

Vartalap is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
